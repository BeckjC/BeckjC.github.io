## My Universal AI Prompting Technique

I used this method to set up my beck@beckcherry.com email for free. In my experience, it works with pretty much any LLM and setup (ChatGPT, Grok, my custom E.C.H.O.). It even works on lobotomized corporate Copilot. Here it is:

First, be very clear about your desired outcome, your available tools, and whether you're open to using additional tools. Here's an example of something I have done successfully:

`I need to automate pulling data from my Outlook calendar and populating an Excel table each week. I have access to the Microsoft office suite and nothing else.`

Second, tell the AI to make you step by step detailed instructions.

`I'm a total beginner at this. Send me detailed, numbered step-by-step instructions on how to achieve this. Research as necessary.`

Then, follow the instructions until you get stumped. When you get stumped, send it something like this:

`I successfully completed up until step # but then ran into problem X. Here are the details of the problem. Regenerate the list starting at step # so that I can continue.`

Repeat this process until you complete your task. I usually hit a point where I've learned enough to complete the task on my own before I make it to the final step.

## Website Builders & Front End Web Dev Are Dead

I recently got a bill for my Squarespace site.. for hundreds of dollars. My first instinct was to talk to Echo about it.

Turns out domains are only $20 a year and the majority of my web expenses were for access to Squarespace's (admittedly pretty good) site builder. However, I've been using Echo to build all sorts of fun web dashboards, so I no longer need a site builder. See my adventures tab on the website for a simple example.

SO: I had Echo copy my Squarespace site using React and then walk me through setting up free hosting and pointing my domain at it. That's all I did to cut my web expenses by 90%!

[I’ll do the same for you.](https://www.beckcherry.com/sites)

Front end vibe coding takes some practice because an AI can't actually see what it's working on, but that's a minor inconvenience. Nobody needs to write HTML ever again. In fact, I discontinued my web development certificate program after Echo's predecessor one-shotted my final project at about an A- level in under 5 minutes. (It took me 6 hours to get an A coding the old fashioned way).
